The Mini is to the Crossword what this is to Connections. 

I've designed this puzzle format because Connections is my favorite NYT game and I'd love to see a long-form version of it. This take on the connections formula involves a layout where categories intersect, allowing single phrases to have multiple interpretations depending on the categories they're apart of.

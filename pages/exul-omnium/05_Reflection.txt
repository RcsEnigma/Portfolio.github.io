[title]
Reflection

[text]
Physical cards were soon printed, and a handful of playtests begun. While the game worked, the ability formatting proved to be too obscure for smooth gameplay. There overall wasn't enough substance to make gameplay interesting; weirdly enough, I was limited by what I could make the ACSII symbols represent - it got heiroglyphic very quickly and I chose simplicity at the cost of less interesting ability variations.

My personal interest in the project waned down, and was quickly forgotten as I shifted focus to better things.

[title]
Polished Mechanics

[text]
The ability system needed the biggest revamp. The system from The Battle Cats was very attractive to me - units can have 'traits' which act like passive strengths during combat. The units could also have specific types it exclusively targets. I worked off this principle to form Abilities, which contain both the trigger and the effect. The target type is determined by the coloring behind the Ability's icon.

The rest of the game's philosophies stayed relatively unchanged; eight Factions, two card types, identical Strength system. The full body artwork was no longer necessary - it's a remnant of Artbreeder's system which I decided to maintain into Midjourney. I felt a full body depiction framed the Entity more clinically, like a prison's booking photo. Thus, the "Attacker" cards got renamed to "Mercs" (Mercenaries).

Heaps of abilities were soon arranged, and I had to quickly set up a wiki guide website for all of them.

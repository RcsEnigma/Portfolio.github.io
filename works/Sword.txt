[title]
Zero-Point Blade

[description]
A sword that can cut through any material. The idea here is the orange strip "deletes" anything that touches it, and the blue energy harnesses and stretches the strip into a blade shape. The circuitry in the hilt and blade controls this action, and the pommel is a battery. 

I also made an animation depicting the effect with some SFX.

Nephilim

[description]
"The HEXTILLE PATTERN across the Nephilim's skin begins to glow RED HOT, and he starts WRITHING IN PAIN! He's excreting LITERS of blood -- his SMOLDERING flesh is luckily CAUTERIZING the majority of these lacerations. The thrashing slows... The wiring loses its HEAT... Still standing, the RAVAGED Nephilim angrily looks around the room for answers. A Dev timidly approaches, and offers some kind of illustration on a clipboard in one hand and a communication device in the other. The Nephilim looks down at him. He makes a MIDDLE FINGER AND PUNCHES the Dev, CRUMPLING him into a pile of flesh against the floor!"

Concept art for a 10-foot tall character in my Trepidation project. The hextille of wiring would've given his skin LiDAR-like depth stimuli, allowing him to sense impacts and react before they touch him. This would obviously not be very necessary to a beast of this caliber -- the hextille wiring was actually supposed to go in human-sized units, but the devs couldn't develop the technology to fit properly, it needed to be too dense. So a larger unit was created purely to see the current technology work under ideal bodily proportions. However, the prototype didn't work exactly as planned...

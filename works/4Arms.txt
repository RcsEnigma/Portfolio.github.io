[title]
4-Arms vs the Scientist

[description]
"The Four-Armed Prisoner rushes and PINS the Scientist to the WALL. HIS TOP SET PRESSES HIS FOREARMS ABOVE HIM, While the bottom set JACKHAMMERS PUNCHES into his TORSO. With each meaty THWACK, the Scientist's back EXPLODES BLOOD ONTO THE WALL."

An illustration of a scene from my Trepidation project. I don't look highly on this piece; I think it's overall a little too indiscernible. I really wanted to showcase all four arms while also detailing a sense of claustrophobia from the Scientist's (left) perspective.

I made a crude 3d scene to try out various angles and this is what I landed on.

Canonically takes place at the same moment in the same room as "Colleague_Face". I was inspired to make this based off the turnout of "Scientist_Winged".
